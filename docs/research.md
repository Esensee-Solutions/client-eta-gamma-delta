# Research: who the Eta is (24 November 2026)

Public sources only. To confirm with the Directiva General before anything here goes live as fact.

Sources: the Eta's own anniversary yearbook (*Anuario Aniversario 92-93*, on FlipBuilder, pages
15–18), the Directiva General's Instagram (`@etagammadelta_dg`), Capítulo Delta's Instagram
(`@delta_etagammadelta`), UPR Mayagüez's student-organizations page, Wikipedia, and the
Facebook groups of the Directiva General and Zona Gamma (titles and descriptions only).

## How they talk about themselves

- **"Gran Sororidad Eta Gamma Delta"**: how chapters write it.
- **"La Eta"** for short; members are **sororas**; the Eta is **"una sola familia"**.
- **"Manos que sirven, voces que inspiran, corazones que lideran"**: the Directiva General's Instagram bio.
- **"Once an Eta, Always an Eta"**: Capítulo Delta's bio.
- **"Todo es mejor con La Preferida"**: a caption on the Directiva General's account.
- **"Desarrolla mujeres líderes, fuertes y empoderadas que se ayudan mutuamente"**: Capítulo Beta (UPRM).
- **Founding principles** (yearbook): **unión, servicio y caridad**, "para bien del estudiante,
  de las universidades y la comunidad".
- 2026 is the **Nonagésimo Octavo (98th) Aniversario**. **2028 is the centenary.**

## Symbols

- **Colours: oro y púrpura**, chosen by the founders. Instagram also uses black (⚫️🟡🟣).
- **Flower: "un radiante capullo de rosa"**: a rosebud, chosen by the founders.
- **Motto: Semper Fidelis**, adopted at the convention of 9 March 1946 (Hotel Condado),
  suggested by Elsa Arce.
- **Crest**: chosen in a 1948 contest between Capítulos Alpha and Gamma; Alpha's design won.
  It's theirs: ask for the file, don't redraw it.
- **Hymn: "Cantemos Eta Gamma Delta"**, by Capítulo Beta, adopted in 1947.
- **Brindis** (the toast) 1957 and 1975, **Invocación** 1974, **Farola** (a song; one verse changed 2019).
- **First flag**: 1959.

## The story, in moments

- **March 1928**: ten young women found the Eta at UPR Río Piedras. First president Margarita Ortiz Toro.
  The first **Casa Alpha**, on calle del Pilar, had 14 residents.
- **1929**: the university recognises it officially.
- **1932**: Círculo de San Juan, today Zona Alpha, the first alumnae chapter.
- **1941**: Capítulo Beta at the Colegio de Mayagüez.
- **1944**: the first Directiva General.
- **1946**: Capítulo Gamma, San Germán. Semper Fidelis adopted.
- **1947**: first convention outside the metro area (Mayagüez); the hymn adopted.
- **1949**: Capítulo Delta, Ponce.
- **1950**: first Srta. Eta Gamma Delta at the convention ball.
- **1957**: "Círculo" becomes "Zona"; first Sorora del Año; first Pre-Convención.
- **1963**: the Casa Club in Santa Rita, Río Piedras ("orgullo de nuestra Sororidad").
- **1981**: first Baile de Debutantes (14 February). First Retorno Delta: la Champañada.
- **2007**: ten sororas in Florida found **Zona Omega**, Orlando. **2015**: Zona Tau, Tampa.
- **2009–10**: the **Fondo Especial de Becas** for sororas who are studying.
- **2010**: Zona Omega joins a Directiva General meeting by Skype for the first time.
- **2018**: the Directiva General approves digitising the archive and the **SISTER system**,
  "que proveerá acceso a la información de las sororas en línea y a tiempo real".

## Traditions worth showing

- **Convención** and **Pre-Convención** every year (Pre-Convención 2025 at Punta Maravayo, Hatillo).
- **Medals**: Medalla de Excelencia (the highest), de Honor, de Servicios Distinguidos,
  Excelencia Académica, Medalla Iris Gelpí. **Copa de Zona del Año** / **Copa de Excelencia**.
- **Juegos que no se Olvidan** (Zona Kappa, since 1995).
- Chapter events: "With Love, Delta", Down syndrome awareness month, Halloween with children,
  joint events with Mu Alpha Phi, Phi Eta Mu, Alpha Beta Chi.

## What this means for the site

1. **The directory is SISTER, finished.** They decided in 2018 they wanted sisters' information
   online and in real time. The site can say so, and the proposal should open with it.
2. **Their voice is warm and proud, not corporate**: "hermandad", "una sola familia", hearts in
   purple and gold. The homepage should sound like that.
3. **The centenary (2028) is a natural hook**: "Camino al Centenario".
4. **The rosebud** is a motif we can draw without touching their crest.
5. Don't publish living sisters' names. Past figures named in their own history (the founders,
   first presidents) are fine once the Directiva General agrees.
